
Data Analyst - Entry Level

We are looking for an entry-level Data Analyst to join our analytics team.

Responsibilities:
- Analyze business data and identify trends and patterns.
- Write SQL queries to extract and transform data.
- Use Python for data cleaning and analysis.
- Create dashboards and reports using Power BI.
- Communicate analytical findings to business stakeholders.
- Perform data validation and quality checks.

Requirements:
- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Data Analytics, Statistics, or a related field.
- Strong SQL skills.
- Basic to intermediate Python knowledge.
- Experience with Power BI or similar visualization tools.
- Understanding of data cleaning and exploratory data analysis.
- Strong analytical and communication skills.
